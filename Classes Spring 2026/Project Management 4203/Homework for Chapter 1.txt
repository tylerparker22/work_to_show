Question:
Like many other professions such as accounting, marketing, and finance, there is a professional organization for project managers. One professional organization for project managers has grown from 93,000 in 2002 to more than 565,000 currently and is the ________.

Answer:
Project Management Institute (PMI)

Writing a policy manual 

Question:

Answer:
Overseeing existing operations

Question:
A series of coordinated, related, multiple projects that continue over an extended time period and are intended to achieve a goal is known as a

Answer:
Program 

Question:
Which of the following is a good example of a program?

Answer:
Developing a new residential area that includes six custom homes 

Question:
A(n) _________ is a series of coordinated, related multiple projects that continue over an extended time intended to achieve a goal.

Answer:
program 

Question:
Specific time, cost, and performance requirements are among the characteristics that separate project management from other endeavors of the organization.

Answer:
True 

Question:
In which stage of the project life cycle is a major portion of the actual project work performed?

Answer:
Executing 

Question:
The establishment of project goals, specifications, and responsibilities usually occurs in the ________ stage of the project life cycle.

Answer:
defining 

Question:
Training the customer, releasing staff, and releasing resources occurs in the ________ stage of the project life cycle.

Answer:
closing 

Question:
Agile PM is appropriate when ________.

Answer:
project scope is unknown or unstable 

Question:
Agile project management involves ________ whereas traditional project management involves ________.

Answer:
iterative and incremental delivery, upfront planning with high predictability 

Question:
Iterative and incremental delivery means ________.

Answer:
the components or features of the project product or service are delivered one at a time throughout the duration of the project 

Question:
Which dimension of project management centers on creating a temporary social system within a larger organizational environment that combines the talents of a divergent set of professionals working to complete the project?

Answer:
Sociocultural 

Question:
Which of these is NOT part of the "technical dimension" of project management?

Answer:
Problem solving 

Question:

sociocultural
