Q2) Explain the concept of present value, net present value, and opportunity cost?
Present value is the value that it is worth today. So you bought gas for $2.00 today, but tomorrow it is $1.50 today the demand was higher so it will be worth $2.00. 

Net present Value is the present value plus the cost of acquiring the asset. You buy something that is worth $1,000 but you get a discount so you pay what the discount is. 

Opportunity cost is how much it is worth at that time.
