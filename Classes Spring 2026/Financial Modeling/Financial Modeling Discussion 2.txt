Financial Modeling Discussion 2

1) Explain the concept of Enterprise Value?

what you would buy the company for including all debt and cash you would recieive. Valuing the companies productive activities. 

2) Explain the three approaches to calculate enterprise value?

1. Accounting: balance sheet operating items on left, financial items on right. 
2. Efficent markets approach: adjusts the accounting based ev by replacing book values with market values especially equity and debt. 
3. Values ev as the present value of expected future free cash flows. 

3) Explain the concept of free cash flow?

this is what happens when you operate and gain revenue. You get money and have free will to do with it. 

4) What are the different approaches to predict the future free cash flows?

1) A simplified approach that uses the firm’s consolidated statement of cash flows to project future free cash flows.

2) A more detailed approach that builds pro forma financial statements and derives free cash flows from them.
